Richard and Saladin never met face to face — despite nearly two years of war, negotiations, and mutual respect. Richard proposed that his sister Joan marry Saladin's brother al-Adil, with Jerusalem as their joint capital; the plan collapsed when Joan refused to marry a Muslim. They exchanged gifts: Richard sent falcons, Saladin sent ice and fruit when Richard fell ill. Baha al-Din, Saladin's biographer, recorded the Crusaders' discipline with admiration. When Richard's horse was killed at Jaffa, Saladin sent him two replacements — during the battle. Chivalry persisted between enemies who could not share a table.
